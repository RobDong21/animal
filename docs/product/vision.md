# Camimi Learn Product Vision

## Objective

Help children aged 5–8 build vocabulary through independent, educational play with animals and words.

## Audience

- Children aged 5–8
- Designed primarily for independent use
- An adult may help during initial use
- iPad landscape is the primary device experience

## Learning approach

- Vocabulary is the primary learning goal.
- Animal types and habitats reinforce vocabulary through meaningful associations.
- Feedback should teach the relevant concept, not merely mark an answer correct or incorrect.
- Animal facts should be concise, age-appropriate, and scientifically defensible.
- Sessions should feel short and complete, with the option to continue playing.

## Product principles

- Make the next action understandable without adult explanation.
- Prefer clarity, consistency, and simplicity over decoration.
- Use encouraging language and treat mistakes as learning opportunities.
- Introduce challenge through richer vocabulary and closer distinctions, not time pressure or punishment.
- Keep controls accessible, child-friendly, and suitable for touch.

## Current boundaries

- Progress does not need to persist between sessions.
- Multiple languages are not currently planned.
- Adaptive difficulty is not required for the first difficulty-level release.
